    The test network, as its record states it
    
  
    Nothing here is invented. The root, its directory, the registrars, their logs and every passport come from the record the test network published. The dust around each registrar is its signed status list, one point per bit, 4,096 each. A dark point is a passport its registrar switched off.
    
  
    You are the verifier. This tab holds the directory, checked against the root's two keys. Each thread that leaves you is a real check of one passport, run here by the verifier the site ships. It contacts nobody while it checks.
    
  
    The timeline replays the record in its own order: each registrar's log, entry by entry, then each change to its status list. The test network keeps a fixed clock, so the order is real and no dates are shown.
