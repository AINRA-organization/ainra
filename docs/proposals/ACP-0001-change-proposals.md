## What it proposes

Every change to AINRA's rules, software or root starts as a numbered, public proposal in this folder; is open for
comment for at least 14 days; and is decided only when every substantiated objection has a written answer. Changes are
of three kinds, soft, hard, and maintenance or security, and each kind has its own path to being switched on. The full
process is [../CHANGE-PROPOSALS.md](../CHANGE-PROPOSALS.md).

## Why

Until now a decision began as the operator's word and was written down afterwards. That is quick, and nobody outside
can see a change coming or object in time. Open networks that have lasted change through proposals anyone can write,
and through rules that old participants can keep working under. AINRA already does the second (rules keyed on a log
position); this adds the first.

## Objections and answers

- *A 14-day comment window slows down urgent fixes.* Security fixes are maintenance releases and do not wait; a
  distrust cutoff in an emergency follows the emergency path (D-098). The window applies to rule changes.
- *One operator still decides.* Yes, until genesis. The proposal makes the decision visible before it is taken, and
  the process does not change when the deciders do.
